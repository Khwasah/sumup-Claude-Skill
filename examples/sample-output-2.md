### Minutes-Style Summary

The Commission approved the agenda and, with one correction (adding Commissioner McCroskey to the Special Committee on Indigent Care), the prior minutes. A resolution reallocating Data Processing Reserve funds to purchase a laptop for the County Clerk's office passed. Commissioner McKee withdrew his motion to sell property near the airport. A resolution to raise the state match local litigation tax to the maximum allowed was amended 17-2 to direct 25% of criminal-case proceeds to the sheriff's department, but the amended resolution failed on a 9-9 roll-call vote (one member not voting). A resolution to raise the wheel tax by $10 to offset state education funding cuts passed 17-2 on first passage, after a motion for previous question also passed 17-2. Announcements covered an upcoming Budget Committee meeting, the next regular commission meeting, and a community chili supper. The meeting was adjourned without objection.
