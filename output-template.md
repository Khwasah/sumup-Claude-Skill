# Output Templates

Two formats — use whichever matches the detected meeting type (see SKILL.md Step 1).

---

## Format A: Business / Informal Meeting

**Decisions Made**
- [Decision, stated plainly]
(If none: "No firm decisions were made in this meeting.")

**Action Items**

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| [Task] | [Name or "unclear — confirm"] | [As stated, or "not specified"] |

(If none: "No action items were assigned.")

**Open Questions / Unresolved**
- [Item raised but not settled]
(If none: "None noted.")

**Announcements**
- [FYI-only items: dates, events]
(If none: "None noted.")

---

### Follow-up Email Draft (Business meetings only)

Subject: Follow-up: [Meeting topic/name]

Hi all,

Thanks for joining today's [meeting name/topic]. Quick recap:

**Decisions:**
- [decision]

**Action items:**
- [Owner]: [task] (by [deadline])

**Still open:**
- [open question]

Let me know if I've missed or misattributed anything.

Best,
[Name]

---

## Format B: Formal / Parliamentary Meeting

**Motions & Votes**

| Motion | Moved by | Seconded by | Vote | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Motion text/summary] | [Name] | [Name] | [Tally, e.g. "17-2" or "voice vote"] | [Passed / Failed / Withdrawn / Amended] |

(If a motion was amended, list the amendment as its own row directly above the amended main motion.)

**Announcements**
- [Meeting dates, events, FYI items]
(If none: "None noted.")

**Open Questions / Unresolved**
- [Procedural questions raised, e.g. vote-threshold clarifications — note how they were resolved if answered on the record]
(If none: "None noted.")

---

### Minutes-Style Summary (Formal meetings, in place of follow-up email)

A short prose paragraph suitable for official minutes: meeting called to order, agenda/minutes approved, each motion and its outcome in sequence, adjournment. Keep it factual and terse, matching the register of formal meeting minutes.
